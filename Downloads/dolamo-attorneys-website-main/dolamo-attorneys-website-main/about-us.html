About Us

Dolamo Attorneys is a legal practice based in eMalahleni (Witbank), Mpumalanga, providing professional legal advice, assistance and representation to individuals, families, businesses and organisations.

We are committed to understanding each client's circumstances and providing clear, practical and professional legal guidance. Whether you require legal advice, assistance with a dispute or representation in legal proceedings, Dolamo Attorneys is here to help you understand your options and navigate the legal process.

  • What documents should I bring to my consultation?

    Bring any documents related to your matter, such as contracts, letters, court documents, identification or correspondence. Unsure? Contact our office first.

  • Will my information be kept confidential?

    Yes, all information shared with our firm is kept strictly confidential in accordance with legal ethics and professional standards.

  • Do you guarantee a successful outcome?

    No responsible attorney can guarantee an outcome. Each case depends on its facts, evidence, applicable law and the decisions of the court or authority.

  • How do I get started?

    Contact Dolamo Attorneys and tell us briefly about your legal matter. We will guide you on the next steps and documents required for a consultation.
