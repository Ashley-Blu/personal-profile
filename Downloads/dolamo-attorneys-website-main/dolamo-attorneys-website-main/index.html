  • What services does Dolamo Attorneys provide?

    Dolamo Attorneys provides legal assistance and representation in matters including litigation, municipal law, divorce, commercial law, third-party claims and criminal law. The firm also assists with estates, wills, eviction matters, and landlord and tenant disputes.

  • How can I arrange a consultation?

    You can contact Dolamo Attorneys by telephone or email to discuss your legal matter and arrange a consultation. It is helpful to provide a brief explanation of your situation and bring any relevant documents to your consultation.

  • When should I contact an attorney?

    It is generally advisable to seek legal advice as soon as you become aware of a legal problem. Early advice can help you understand your rights, responsibilities and available options before the matter becomes more complicated.

  • What should I bring to my consultation?

    Bring any documents that are relevant to your matter. Depending on your circumstances, these may include contracts, court documents, correspondence, identification documents, police documents, medical records or previous legal documents.
