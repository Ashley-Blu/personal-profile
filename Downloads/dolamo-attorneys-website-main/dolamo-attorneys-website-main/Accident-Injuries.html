Criminal Law

Criminal law concerns offences where a person is accused of breaking the law. Being arrested or charged with a criminal offence can be a serious matter, and it is important to understand your rights and the legal process from the outset.

Dolamo Attorneys provides legal advice and representation to clients facing criminal charges. Our services may include assistance with bail applications, court appearances and the defence of criminal charges, depending on the circumstances of the matter.

How We Can Help

If you have been arrested, charged with an offence or required to appear in court, we can assist you in understanding the charges and the legal process. We can provide legal advice, assist with bail applications where applicable and represent you during the relevant criminal proceedings.

Faq About This Service

  • What should I do if I have been arrested?

    You should seek legal assistance as soon as possible. Avoid discussing the details of your case publicly and ask an attorney to advise you on your rights and available options.

  • Can you assist with bail applications?

    Yes. Depending on the circumstances of the matter, we can assist with bail applications and related criminal proceedings.

  • Can you represent me if I have been charged with a crime?

    Yes. We provide legal representation and advice to clients facing criminal charges.

  • What happens if I receive a criminal summons?

    Do not ignore it. Contact an attorney promptly so that you understand the charge, the court process and what you need to do next.
